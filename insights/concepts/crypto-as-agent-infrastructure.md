# Crypto as Agent Infrastructure

**Crypto is to agents what property rights were to capitalism.** Not the energy source. Not the machines. The legal fiction that made the whole thing work. The thing that turned raw capability into an economy.

## Why the Analogy Holds

Without property rights, markets don't function regardless of how much energy you have. Without crypto, agents can think but they can't do business.

Agents need to transact. They need identity. They need to hold value, make payments, prove ownership, coordinate with other agents without a human cosigning every interaction. Traditional finance has no concept of a non-human economic actor. Try opening a bank account for a Claude instance.

Crypto rails give agents economic agency: wallets they can hold, transactions they can sign, protocols they can interact with programmatically. No KYC for a smart contract call. No business hours. No wire transfer delays. The only financial infrastructure that treats an API call and a human click as equivalent.

## The Sidestep

This framing sidesteps the usual crypto debate entirely. Not defending the technology. Describing a structural requirement. Agents need to own, transact, and coordinate. That's an architecture problem. Crypto happens to be the only solution that exists.

## What Agents Need

- **Wallets:** self-custodied, programmatically controlled
- **Signing:** cryptographic proof of agent identity and intent
- **Programmable transactions:** smart contracts that execute without human intervention
- **Token-gated access:** credentials and permissions encoded as tokens
- **Coordination primitives:** DAOs, multisigs, voting mechanisms for multi-agent systems

## What Agents Don't Need

DeFi yield farming, speculative trading, NFT art markets. The agent economy needs infrastructure, not casinos.

## The Governance Question

If crypto is property rights, what's the equivalent of "rule of law"? Smart contracts provide enforcement. Protocol governance provides evolution. But the agent economy will also need dispute resolution, liability frameworks, and bankruptcy analogs. These don't exist yet.

## Historical Parallel

Property rights in early capitalism faced similar skepticism. How do you "own" land that was there before you? The answer was institutional: courts, registries, norms. Agent property rights will need similar institutional scaffolding.

## Moravec's Automated Corporation

The automated corporation is DAOs of agents and smart contracts. Agents and contracts ARE the governed assets. Crypto is to agents what property rights were to capitalism: the enabling fiction that makes everything else possible.
