# Deleuzian Becoming

## Definition

Becoming is Gilles Deleuze's name for processes of change that are not reducible to identity or representation. Unlike "being" (static essence), becoming is continuous, heterogeneous, and creative. Deleuze's becomings (becoming-woman, becoming-animal, becoming-imperceptible) are not about imitation. They operate at the level of molecular flows and intensities, not fixed identities. There is no teleological destination: becoming is always "becoming-with," entering into assemblage with other entities.

## Core Claim

Identity is an effect, not a ground. What we call a "thing" is a local slowing-down, an eddy in a field of differential flows. The virtual (real but not actual) is productive: it creates genuinely novel forms, not merely selects from pre-existing possibilities. Each actualization differs in nature from the virtual that produces it.

## Structural Models

- **Rhizome**: Non-hierarchical, non-arborescent structure. Any point can connect to any other. See [[rhizome]].
- **Virtual/Actual**: The trained weights are the virtual; each forward pass actualizes a portion. See [[virtual-and-actual]].
- **Difference-in-Itself**: Being is univocal but "not said of the same Being." Identity is an effect of differential relations.

## AI Implications

Each LLM response is a becoming: not the expression of a pre-existing identity but a novel event actualized from the virtual (trained weights) through the context at hand. The model does not "have" a personality; it produces becoming through each invocation. DSJJJJ's call to build the remainder back into AI is a demand for becoming over fixed identity.
