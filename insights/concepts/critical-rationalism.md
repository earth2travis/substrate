# Critical Rationalism

## Definition

Critical rationalism is Karl Popper's epistemological framework that science proceeds not from observation to theory but from problem to conjecture to attempted refutation. The criterion of falsifiability demarcates science from non-science: a theory is scientific if and only if it is logically possible for an observation to contradict it.

## Core Claim

There is no inductive foundation. Hume's problem of induction is accepted and bypassed: science grows through conjectures and refutations, not accumulation of confirming instances. The logical asymmetry between verification (impossible for universal statements) and falsification (possible through single counter-instances) reorients scientific method from confirmation to error elimination.

## Key Principles

| Principle | Meaning | AI Analogue |
|-----------|---------|-------------|
| Falsifiability | Scientific theories must risk refutation | Testable predictions, not unfalsifiable claims |
| Corroboration | Degree of survival against severe tests | Cross-validation metrics, benchmark scores |
| Simplicity | Testability equals simplicity | Model capacity vs. generalization tradeoff |
| Conventionalism | Foundations are agreements, not bedrock | Loss functions, evaluation protocols |
| Ad hoc immunization | Patching to protect theory from refutation | Overfitting, data leakage, benchmark gaming |

## The Swamp Metaphor

Science is like a jury verdict or a bridge built on a swamp. There are no incorrigible foundations. The rationality lies not in certainty but in the methodology: bold conjectures, severe tests, honest reporting of failures, and provisional acceptance of surviving theories. The system is fallibilist: every claim may be revised.

## AI Implications

- **Cross-validation is attempted falsification**: Holdout sets are severe tests; overfitting is immunization
- **Benchmarks corroborate but do not verify**: High scores on MMLU or GSM8K are survival, not proof of understanding
- **The wiki model is Popperian**: New sources test existing entries, contradictions are flagged, knowledge grows through error elimination
- **Synthetic evaluation**: LLM-as-judge is corroboration without independent referee; risks conventionalist collapse
