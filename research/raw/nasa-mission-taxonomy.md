## Observation
This taxonomy reveals how NASA thinks about missions as multi-dimensional objects. A single mission can be tagged with:
- One status (Active/Future/Past)
- One or more types (e.g., "Rover" + "Helicopter & Rotorcraft" for Mars 2020)
- One or more targets (e.g., "Mars and its moons")
- One or more programs (e.g., "Mars Exploration" + "Planetary Science")

This structured metadata approach enables precise filtering, cross-referencing, and discovery across NASA's portfolio of 100+ missions.
