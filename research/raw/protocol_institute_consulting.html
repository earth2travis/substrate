Consulting

Protocol Institute staff and members are available for consulting engagements on a range of protocol-related challenges, including protocol design and analysis, organizational protocolization, governance, and strategy. Where institute resources are of value, consulting engagements can be structured to include access to them.

Please enquire about engagements with individual consultants directly, using the contact link on their card.

If you're not sure what kind of expertise might be relevant, please contact PI Managing Director Timber Stinson-Schroff.

If you'd like to be added to this directory, please submit your profile via this form. This directory is mainly for individuals who are, or have been, active participants in PI activities such as special interest groups, magazine publishing, and past grant programs of the Summer of Protocols program. If you are new to PI and its work, please spend time participating in our activities on Discord before applying.
