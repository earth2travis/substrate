# Adoniram Judson: The Bible Translator as Martyr and Scholar

## The Work

Adoniram Judson (1788–1850) was an American Baptist missionary who worked in Burma for nearly 40 years. He translated the entire Bible into Burmese and established numerous Baptist churches.

## The Linguistic Achievement

Judson's Burmese Bible was the first complete Bible in that language. He also compiled a Burmese-English dictionary and a Burmese grammar. The dictionary remained the standard reference for over a century.

He arrived in Burma at age 25. The first years produced no converts. Judson was imprisoned during the First Anglo-Burmese War (1824–1826) and suffered torture. His wife Ann died of illness. Yet he continued translating.

## The Scale

Judson's work catalyzed the Triennial Convention, the first national Baptist organization for missionary work. His example inspired generations of American missionaries to see linguistic work as central, not ancillary, to mission.

## The Pattern

The missionary-linguist as long-term infrastructure builder. Judson spent decades creating the linguistic foundation (dictionary, grammar, Bible translation) upon which all subsequent Burmese Christianity was built. The dictionary outlived the missionary. The grammar outlived the church.
