## 1. The Protocol Institute

**Source:** Website protocol-institute.org
**URL:** https://protocol-institute.org/

An independent research organization dedicated to the study of protocols: the rules, procedures, and coordination structures that shape how individuals, organizations, and systems interact.

Evolved from the Summer of Protocols (2023-2024), funded by the Ethereum Foundation. Brought together 80+ researchers across philosophy, organizational theory, cryptography, urban infrastructure.

Initiatives:
- **Protocolized** — editorial and publishing arm
- **C3PO** — RAG-based conversational research assistant on Protocol Institute's full corpus (12,000+ vectors). Available as MCP server.
- **Special Interest Groups** — biweekly groups on memory, formal theory, organization, storytelling
- **Protocols for the Long Now** — collaboration with Long Now Foundation
- **Protocol Symposium 2026** — September 2026

Team:
- Timber Stinson-Schroff, Managing Director
- Venkatesh Rao, Director of Research
- James Langdon, Executive Editor, Protocolized
- Tim Beiko, Chair, Advisory Board

Network (PIN):
- Metropolitan Water District Innovation Unit
- University of Messina IoT Lab
- NYU Shanghai Trust Experience Lab

Consulting available on protocol design, organizational protocolization, governance, strategy.

**Sivart note:** Venkatesh Rao's involvement makes this automatically relevant. The Protocol Institute is doing for "protocols" what we are trying to do for "missions" — building the conceptual vocabulary and community. Worth following their work on formal protocol theory.

## 4. Palantir Ontology

**Source:** X post by @PalantirTech (2026-04-27)
**URL:** https://x.com/PalantirTech/status/2049136883528011954

The Ontology is a system designed to represent decisions in an enterprise, not simply data.

Four components of any operational decision:
1. **Data** — information leveraged to make the decision
2. **Logic** — heuristics and computational processes that evaluate a decision
3. **Action** — orchestration and execution of the chosen decision
4. **Security** — assurance that the decision complies with policies

Traditional data architectures don't capture reasoning or action. Conventional analytics don't contextualize computation within lived reality.

**Sivart note:** Palantir's Ontology maps directly to Mission Contract concepts. Data + Logic + Action + Security = our six handoffs in enterprise form. Palantir has proven this architecture at scale in government and commercial contexts.

## 5. Eureka Labs

**Source:** Website eurekalabs.ai
**URL:** https://eurekalabs.ai/

Founded July 16, 2024. Building a new kind of school that is AI native.

Concept: The teacher designs course materials, but they are supported, leveraged, and scaled with an AI Teaching Assistant optimized to guide students. Teacher + AI symbiosis running an entire curriculum.

**Sivart note:** Education is a domain where agent systems will have massive impact. The "teacher + AI symbiosis" model is a governance pattern: human sets direction, agent scales execution. Similar to how we think about Mission Contracts.

## Summary: Protocol Themes

1. **Protocols are a category worth studying** — The Protocol Institute is building the academic and conceptual foundation. Venkatesh Rao is the leading thinker here.
2. **Decision-centric architectures** — Palantir's Ontology proves that framing systems around decisions (not data) works at enterprise scale.
3. **Real-time feedback** — The Dot Collector and similar tools create the observability layer that agent systems need.
4. **Education as a testbed** — Eureka Labs shows how human-AI collaboration models can be prototyped in lower-stakes domains.
