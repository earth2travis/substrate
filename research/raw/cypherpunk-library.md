# The Cypherpunk Library

**URL:** https://www.cypherpunkbooks.com/
**Date captured:** 2026-06-09

A curated digital library collecting foundational cypherpunk texts, manifestos, essays, and books. The site also links to Anna's Archive and LibGen for broader access to the full collection.

- **The Crypto Anarchist Manifesto** by Timothy C. May
- **A Declaration of the Independence of Cyberspace** by John Perry Barlow
- **Why I Wrote PGP** by Philip Zimmermann
- **The Conscience of a Hacker** by Loyd Blankenship (The Mentor)

## Relevance

The cypherpunk tradition — code as speech, cryptography as a human right, minimizing trust and maximizing individual sovereignty — is direct lineage for the project's open-source and autonomy commitments. The partnership carries this DNA: build in the open, release without permission, design systems where trust is minimized and individual sovereignty is maximized.
