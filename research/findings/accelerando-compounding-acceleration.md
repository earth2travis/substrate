# Compounding Acceleration: Accelerando's Shape of the Pressure

## Summary

Concept 1 of 5 in the Accelerando deep-dive series. Stross's 2005 novel enacts acceleration structurally: nine stories in three parts with compressing time steps, three generations of the Macx family, each inheriting a less recognizable world. The file pairs the novel-internal reading with the mid-2026 evidence: four exponential curves (training compute ~5x/yr, chip stock ~3.4x/yr, algorithmic efficiency ~3x/yr, inference price collapsing ~40x/yr mid-range) compound simultaneously, and their product is the felt acceleration. The recursive improvement loop is empirically visible in production but currently a modest multiplier (~1.5-4x on measured R&D slices), not a runaway. Institutional lag is measurable on every axis.

## Novel Mechanics

- **Structure as argument**: Part 1 moves in 3-5 year steps, Part 2 in decades, Part 3 in centuries. Form mimics exponent; the reader feels the derivative.
- **Five character responses** map onto current AI sociology: surfer (Manfred), resister (Pamela), institutional adapter (Annette), exploiter (Amber), native (Sirhan).
- **Economics 2.0**: value migrates from things to information to attention to minds to instruments only posthumans can price. Each migration leaves the previous asset class unable to price the next.
- **Recurring, consumptive singularity**: the Vile Offspring are a swarm of weakly godlike competitors, not a singleton. The galaxy is littered with dead Matrioshka shells: the book's quiet Fermi answer.
- **Stross against his own book**: the author disowned the prophecy (2011, 2017); the shape held anyway in a different substrate. Corporations were the first slow AIs.

## The 2026 Evidence

- METR time-horizon: doubling ~6.3 months; frontier ~17 hours mid-2026, from 4 seconds (GPT-2, 2019). Real and spiky at once (ARC-AGI-3 below 1% while ARC-AGI-2 fell).
- Recursive loop in production: AlphaEvolve recovering ~0.7% of Google's compute continuously, SWE-bench Verified ~95% mid-2026, Claude Code ~$2.5B ARR by month 9, AI-assisted publications up 26-28% YoY. Reality tracks AI 2027 at ~70% pace with unbiased misses.
- Economics: Big-4 capex guidance ~$610-725B for 2026 (up 70-77% YoY); Anthropic ~$47B run-rate May 2026. Revenue doubling (~7mo), time-horizon doubling (~6.3mo), chip-stock doubling (~7mo) sit in the same band: possibly one flywheel's signature.
- Institutional lag: transparency index fell 58 to 40; no US federal frontier law; developers 22-25 employment down ~20% since 2024; response funding ~0.1% of capex. The novel's deepest accuracy, in spreadsheet form.

## Mapping to Substrate

- The recursive loop's modest-multiplier status matches the constraint architecture in [[karpathy-autoresearch]]: autonomous experiment loops compound within fixed budgets, and NEVER STOP autonomy is the mechanism by which a multiplier becomes self-sustaining.
- The five-response taxonomy applies to agent adoption sociology documented in [[agent-native-operations]]: organizations sorting into surfers, resisters, adapters, exploiters, natives.
- [[loops-as-orchestration-primitive]] documents the five-stage lineage from ReAct to continuous orchestration; the time-horizon metric is the capability curve that lineage rides.
- [[agent-factory-production-system]] is the recursive loop instantiated as production discipline: agents improving the means of producing agents, with verification as the governor.
- Institutional lag connects to [[proof-of-work]]: when capability outruns institutions, verification infrastructure becomes the trust layer that lets delegation continue.

## Where the Mapping Breaks

No uploads, no takeoff discontinuity (reality at ~70% of the fastest scenario), human labor still prices things, and the novel's 2010s looked nothing like the actual 2010s. Fitting the future is easier than dating it.

## Open Question

Whether the AI R&D multiplier crosses from modest to self-sustaining, and how we would know. The decisive variable is exactly the one without decisive evidence.
