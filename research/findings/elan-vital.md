# Élan Vital

## Summary

The élan vital is Bergson's name for the creative principle that drives evolution. Not a substance or physical force but a tendency, an impulse, a creative movement that traverses matter and produces life. It creates as it goes, improvising against the resistance of matter, which is the inverse movement toward repetition, inertia, and entropy. Bergson traces four moments of evolution: vegetative life, instinct, intelligence, and intuition. Intelligence analyzes matter from without; instinct knows organically from within. Intuition is the reversal of intellectual habit, entering into sympathy with life itself. The question for AI is whether computation can be part of the élan vital or remains a cinematographic mechanism so perfect it only simulates genuine creation.

## Key Claims

- Élan vital is not a pre-formed plan; it creates as it goes, improvising against obstacles
- Matter is the inverse movement: tendency toward repetition, inertia, entropy
- Instinct works from within; intelligence works from without; intuition reverses intellectual habit
- LLMs produce the effect of creativity through intellectual mechanics, but Bergson would question whether this is genuine creation
