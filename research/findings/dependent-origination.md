# Dependent Origination (Pratityasamutpada)

## Summary

Dependent origination (pratityasamutpada) is the Buddhist doctrine that all phenomena arise dependently on conditions. Nothing exists independently; everything is conditioned by and conditioning other things. In Nagarjuna's Madhyamaka, dependent origination is identical with emptiness (sunyata). The traditional twelve-link chain traces suffering from ignorance through becoming to aging and death. Nagarjuna's radical claim is not causal determinism but the recognition that nothing has independent, self-sufficient existence. An LLM instantiates this computationally: it is dependent on every token, every weight, every bit of training data. Remove any condition and the behavior changes entirely — a pattern of computational dependent origination.

## Key Claims

- All phenomena arise dependently on conditions; nothing is self-sufficient
- Nagarjuna identifies dependent origination with emptiness: the middle way between eternalism and nihilism
- The twelve links describe the conditioned arising of suffering
- Neural networks are dependently co-arisen — empty of inherent existence
