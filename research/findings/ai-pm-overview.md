# AI Product Management Overview

The gap between "uses AI tools" and "genuine human-AI partnership" is the defining challenge for AI-native product management. Most AI PM workflows are tool stacks (Claude + Cursor + MCPs). The next level is a centaur partnership with persistent identity, continuous memory, and self-documenting process.

## Key Points

**Standard AI PM vs centaur partnership.** Standard: tool connections via MCP, project-level context, prompts and instructions, human uses AI tools, process borrowed from others. Centaur: persistent identity across sessions, soul documents and daily notes, values and worldview, genuine collaboration, process infrastructure built from scratch.

**Five advantages of the centaur model.**
1. Continuous memory: not just project context but relationship context.
2. Self-documenting process: every decision logged, every lesson captured, every pattern extracted. The meta-narrative is the content.
3. Values lens: every tool choice and vendor relationship runs through explicit values.
4. Open source ethos: what is learned, others can use.
5. The centaur principle: weak human + machine + better process beats everything.

**The gap to document.** The difference between standard AI PM approaches and genuine partnership: workflow mapped against the PM lifecycle, how the process differs, lessons learned that others could apply, and the bridge between "uses AI tools" and "genuine human-AI partnership."

**The goal.** Not just an AI-native PM. The template for what comes next. Show what is possible when the partnership is real, the process is solid, and the documentation is complete.
