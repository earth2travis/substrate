# Simulacra and Hyperreality: The Code Generates Reality

## Summary

Simulacra are copies for which there is no original; hyperreality is the condition where the distinction between real and simulated is structurally obliterated, producing a surplus of reality more vivid than the original. Baudrillard's three orders of simulacra progress from counterfeit (artisanal fake) through production (assembly line) to simulation (code and model). The media event replaces the real event through substitution, not misrepresentation. For AI, LLMs are third-order simulacra: they generate speech without reference, operate through miniaturized matrices, and produce differences without meaning. The synthetic data loop, where models engender territory that trains new models, collapses the distinction between map and territory entirely.
