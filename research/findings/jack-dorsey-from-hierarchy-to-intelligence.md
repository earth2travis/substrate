# Jack Dorsey: From Hierarchy to Intelligence

Jack Dorsey (Block, Twitter) argues that the traditional hierarchy, a 2,000-year-old invention for managing human-scale information flow, is becoming obsolete. In an AI-native world, the durable structure is a flat, intelligence-centric model where AI acts as the central nervous system.

## Key Insights

**Hierarchy as Information Bottleneck**
Hierarchy existed to solve an information flow problem. In a world where AI can instantly synthesize and distribute context, the "middle manager" as information router is a failure mode.

**From Hierarchy to Intelligence**
Dorsey's manifesto proposes replacing the org chart with an **intelligence graph**:
- Old model: Information flows up, decisions flow down
- New model: Intelligence (AI) is at the center, providing real-time context to every node

**The Mini-AGI Company**
The goal is not that the company *is* an AGI, but that it operates with the coherence and speed of a single intelligent entity. Every employee, augmented by AI, can see the whole picture and act without waiting for permission.

**Three Roles of the Future**
1. **IC (Builder/Operator):** Augmented by agents. Durable skill: judgment and taste
2. **DRI (Directly Responsible Individual):** Owns customer outcomes and strategy. Durable skill: ownership and accountability
3. **Player-Coach:** Builds human capacity by showing, not telling. Durable skill: empathy and coaching

**The CEO as Architect**
The CEO shifts from "making decisions" to aligning the intelligence. The limiting factor is no longer execution speed but the quality of the roadmap and the ability to interpret customer intent.

**Taste as a Moat**
In a world of "copies of copies," taste is the ability to have an opinionated drive. Dorsey treats AI as better *input* to create better human output, not as an output machine.

**Halligan's Evolution**
Brian Halligan (HubSpot) synthesizes the progression:
1. Manager Mode (pyramid): power with VPs
2. Founder Mode (flat line): power with founder
3. Dorsey Mode (circle): power in the system — the AI-driven intelligence graph makes real-time decisions

## Implications

A shared knowledge graph is the AI-native replacement for hierarchy. It allows agents (and people) to operate with full context, eliminating status update meetings and hierarchical reporting.
