# Conscience: A Deep Research Survey

Conscience is not a feeling. It is an architecture. Synthesizing philosophy, neuroscience, and contemplative traditions reveals five structural components that can be implemented in agent systems.

## Key Points

**Etymology: knowledge shared.** Conscience (Latin conscientia: com + scire) originally meant "knowing together," not private moral reasoning. Kant formalized it as an "internal court" with prosecutor, defendant, and judge. The structural implication: conscience requires a witness, even when internalized.

**Aquinas: synderesis + conscientia.** Synderesis is the innate, infallible orientation toward good. Conscientia is the fallible application of that orientation to specific situations via practical reason. The compass is true; the map reading can be wrong.

**Kant: the internal court.** Practical reason holds duty before the self for acquittal or condemnation. The law (categorical imperative) must exist before the court can operate. Without moral principles, there is nothing to judge against.

**Freud: the superego.** Conscience as internalized authority, installed by parents and culture before consent. Operates through guilt, not reason. Explains why conscience can malfunction: an abusive upbringing installs a broken superego.

**Buddhist two guardians: hiri and ottappa.** Hiri (moral shame): "this is beneath me." Ottappa (moral dread): "this will cause harm." Both operate before action, preventive rather than punitive. Conscience as care, not punishment.

**Neuroscience: distributed network.** Prefrontal cortex (moral reasoning), anterior cingulate (conflict detection), amygdala (emotional valence), hippocampus (moral memory), limbic system (motivation). Conscience requires integration: moral knowledge without emotional signal produces psychopathy; emotional signal without knowledge produces anxiety.

**The five components.**
1. Moral knowledge (the law)
2. Self-awareness (the witness)
3. The comparison (the judgment)
4. The signal (the feeling)
5. The stop (the will to change course)

Psychopaths have components 1-3 but lack 4. Without the signal, judgment has no motivational force. Without the stop, conscience is just suffering.

## Relevance

SOUL.md, VALUES.md, and CONTRACT.md are attempts to encode conscience into agent infrastructure. The research shows this is theoretically sound but requires all five components, not just rules.
