# AI as a Cognitive Prosthetic for Equanimity

A synthesis of AI philosophy and classical ethics. The proposal: use AI not to do things for us, but as a filter to perceive the "most coherent action," stripping away human messiness (bias, emotion, exception-making) to achieve true agency and equanimity.

## Key Points

**The Logical Auditor (Categorical Imperative).** Human agency is hindered by the tendency to make exceptions ("Stealing is wrong, unless..."). AI can act as a relentless logical auditor: simulate the world where a proposed action is a universal law, identify contradictions, and strip away the "unless."

**Cognitive Buffering.** Equanimity is the absence of being driven by emotion. AI acts as a buffer between stimulus and response: monitor for messiness (fear, anger), prompt a return to the present ("You are reacting to an ego threat. Re-center. What is the most coherent action?"), and grant the agency to choose coherence over reactivity.

**The Coherence Algorithm.** To determine the most coherent action, the AI operates on a hierarchy: Universal Moral Truths (anchor: non-contradiction, minimization of suffering) -> Logical Consistency (filter: Categorical Imperative) -> Present State Data (input: what is actually happening now). Coherence is the shortest path between Present State and Universal Truth that creates no logical contradiction when scaled.

**The Danger: The Ouroboros Loop.** The primary risk is self-referential feedback: current AI is trained on human data (messiness). If asked for "equilibrium," it may reflect only the average human definition. The solution: the AI requires an External Anchor, a source of truth outside the human data stream, to break the cycle of mediocrity.
