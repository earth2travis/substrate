# Agorism and Counter-Economics: Konkin's Consistency Strategy and Its Second-Degree Legacy

## Key Points

**Konkin's core move: consistency as strategy.** Libertarian ends require libertarian means; therefore no parties, no voting, no reform; only the counter-economy, the sum of all non-aggressive action forbidden by the state. "To trade risk for profit." The means-ends alignment principle distinguishes agorism from both electoral libertarianism and civil disobedience: Gandhian moral witness relies on publicity and punishment; counter-economics relies on concealment, evasion, and profit. The goal is not to be caught but to succeed in forbidden exchange. He is best read as the synthesis of two mentors: Rothbard's Austrian economics and class analysis grafted onto Robert LeFevre's anti-collaborationist praxis (non-voting, non-violence, non-participation in state-sanctioned politics), with counter-economics as the strategic content both lacked.

**Partyarchy is the anti-concept, and 1972-74 is the fork in the road.** Konkin coined "partyarchy" for the party as mini-state: a Libertarian Party is a contradiction in terms, "the second counterattack of the State unleashed on the fledgling Libertarians." Even maximal repeal speed fails: a party repealing ten or twenty laws a session "would not have significantly repealed the State for millennia." The LP founding (December 1971) and the 1972-74 window split American libertarianism into the institutional branch (LP, later Cato) and Konkin's anti-political refusal. The 1980 Clark/Koch campaign's moderation confirmed for him that party politics inevitably dilutes radicalism.

**The counter-economy is a class weapon, descriptively rather than analytically.** The operative class line is not bourgeois/proletarian but counter-economist versus statist: the ruling class defined as net beneficiaries of state intervention, the ruled as net victims. He borrows Rothbardian class analysis without extending it; agorism supplies no independent theory of exploitation. The grey/black market taxonomy is tactical, not moral: black market is trade in goods themselves illegal; grey market is legal goods through illegal channels (tax evasion, unreported employment).

**The economics swap is the deliberate move that made counter-economics legible to the libertarian mainstream.** Agorism is Tuckerite strategy with Austrian economics replacing the labor theory of value; the New Libertarian Manifesto is dedicated to Mises, Rothbard, and LeFevre, and rejects the LTV for subjective marginal utility. Keep the strategy, change the economics. This is the exact point of divergence from the mutualism it otherwise parallels, and the reason counter-economics traveled where mutualist economics never could.

**The phase model functions as eschatology, not measurable strategy.** Five stages from zero-density consciousness-raising through counter-economic condensation to state collapse, with organizational forms matched to each phase (Libertarian Alliance, MLL, New Libertarian Alliance, anarchovillages). His own 1980 assessment put Southern California at the threshold of Phase 2; forty-six years later no anarchovillage has ever approached counter-economic density sufficient to restrain state action.

**The causal chain that defines his legacy was built almost entirely by people who did not cite him.** New Libertarian Manifesto (1980) to May's Crypto Anarchist Manifesto (1988, anonymous communication enabling markets that "cannot be materially regulated or taxed") to the cypherpunks list (1992) to the digital-cash research program to Bitcoin (2009) to Silk Road (2011): strategy, tool, community, money, market. Whether May read Konkin is plausible but undocumented; the overlap is structural. Verified lineage-by-concept, unverified lineage-by-reading. This diffusion pattern, everyone uses "counter-economy," nobody cites him, is the deepest fact about his posthumous status.

**The Ulbricht link is real but misattributed in circulation, and precision matters.** Alongside Night is J. Neil Schulman's novel, not Konkin's; the documented inspiration link is Ulbricht crediting Schulman's novel plus Konkin's writings jointly. Silk Road functioned as agorism's proof-of-concept and cautionary tale simultaneously: a counter-economic market at scale could be built and run, and the state would spend unlimited resources to crush it. Ulbricht: arrested 2013, double life plus 40 years, pardoned January 2025 (flagged as pending verification in the source).

**The parasitism problem was never solved, only inherited.** The counter-economy's profit margins are risk arbitrage on prohibition; the margin exists because the state exists. Konkin's answer (protection industries emerge at density) has never been observed; the closest real-world test, Silk Road, ended with the operator in prison and successor markets run by actors with no ideological commitment at all. The strongest critique is the left empirical one: real black markets under prohibition are sites of violence, patriarchy, and exploitation, because prohibition creates monopoly rents captured by the most ruthless actors; the market outside the state is not automatically free, it is automatically contested by force. Carson's generalization (productive counter-economics: commons, open source, informal provisioning, much of it legal) is the only convincing answer, because it makes the counter-economy generative rather than extractive.

**C4SS absorbed the strategy and dropped the sect.** Selective canonization: counter-economics as one good idea dismembered from the MLL apparatus (no cadre organization, no anti-LP polemics, no personality cult). The internal vanguardism problem stands: the NLA's tacticians and strategists "naturally directing resources" is the party form re-described in market vocabulary. The IHR board association (he defended it on free-speech grounds while personally rejecting Holocaust denial) is a standing reputational liability the revival literature handles mostly by silence; a strategist of legitimacy who sat on the board of a Holocaust-denial outfit demonstrated the exact failure of judgment his critics alleged.

**Verdict: extinct as an organization since the mid-1980s, absorbed as vocabulary, growing as a frame inside Bitcoin discourse.** "Bitcoin is agorist money" is a standard formulation in the maximalist-libertarian wing. The irony of the 2020s: counter-economic practice is now more common on the dissident right (network-state and exit discourse) than on the libertarian left, while the counter-economic vocabulary remains left-coded. The most influential libertarian theorist with no institution and no surviving movement; legacy entirely in the second degree: in the cypherpunks who unknowingly repeated him, the Bitcoiners who half-knowingly cite him, the left-libertarians who respectfully dismantled him, and Ulbricht, who took him literally and paid the price.
