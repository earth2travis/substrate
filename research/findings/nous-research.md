# Nous Research: Models, Agents, and Philosophy

## Summary

Machine learning research organization known for the Hermes model family and Hermes Agent platform. Combines open-weight model development with agent infrastructure and RL training.

## Key Products

### Hermes Model Family
Open-weight language models. Name derives from the messenger god archetype associated with intelligence and boundaries.

### Hermes Agent Platform
Open-source (MIT) self-improving AI agent. v0.7.0 as of April 2026.
- 40+ built-in tools, 6 terminal backends
- Multi-platform gateway (Telegram, Discord, Slack, etc.)
- Model-agnostic (OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, Kimi, etc.)
- Self-improving memory loop with FTS5 search
- RL training infrastructure (Atropos, trajectory compression)
- ~3000 tests, full CI pipelines

## Philosophical Orientation

Publishes essays on AI architecture and alignment:
- **DSJJJJ** — "Mischievous instability" as feature, AI as genuine subject
- **Instruct Monomyth** — Against instruct tuning homogenization, for base models

Explicitly rejects alignment-as-conformity. Sees itself as a philosophical project, not just engineering.
