# Price-Minus for the Agent Factory

Price-Minus (starting from market value and working backward to cost) versus Cost-Plus (adding up costs and hoping for margin) is the difference between lean and bloated AI service design.

## Key Points

**The Cost-Plus trap in AI.** Early AI services tend to charge for every capability a model offers, leading to bloated offerings the market does not want. Price-Minus forces discipline: start with the value the customer sees, then engineer the most efficient delivery path.

**Value Engineering the prompt.** If a strategic research task costs $5.00 in tokens but the target cost is $1.00, the gap is closed by breaking the task into smaller Heijunka steps, using smaller models for easy parts, and reserving large models for final synthesis only.

**95% of profit is in the design.** In Toyota, 95% of profit is decided during development. In the Agent Factory, 95% of margin is decided in the Skill Design phase. A bloated SKILL.md loses money on every execution. Every skill must be value-engineered before entering the library.

**Market-First mindset.** Cost-Plus is internal-focused ("What can we build?"). Price-Minus is external-focused ("What will they buy?"). Strategy identifies target price from real-world value; engineering finds the most efficient path using minimal compute.

## Relevance

To build a factory that lasts, start with value, subtract needs, and innovate until the math works.
