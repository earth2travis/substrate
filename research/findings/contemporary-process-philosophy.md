# Contemporary Process Philosophy: Deleuze, Stengers, Rescher, and the Process Turn

## Summary

Process philosophy has experienced a contemporary flowering across continental, analytic, and interdisciplinary domains. Deleuze radicalizes difference-in-itself as ontologically primary, replacing the possible/real with the virtual/actual and developing the rhizome as an anti-hierarchical model of becoming. Isabelle Stengers extends Whitehead into cosmopolitics and the ecology of practices, insisting that non-human entities have genuine agency. Nicholas Rescher provides formalized analytic process metaphysics with process logic and categorial processualism. Katherine Hayles applies process frameworks to posthuman cognition and cognitive assemblages. The debate with Object-Oriented Ontology (Harman) and new materialism (Bennett, Barad) marks process vs. substance as the central contemporary metaphysical question.
