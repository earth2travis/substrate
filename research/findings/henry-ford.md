# Henry Ford: Mass Production Pioneer

## Summary

American industrialist who pioneered mass production through the moving assembly line, making automobiles affordable to the masses. His methods established the dominant industrial paradigm for 60+ years.

## Key Innovation

The moving assembly line (1913):
- Production time per car: 12.5 hours → 93 minutes
- By 1914, a car every 93 minutes at Highland Park plant
- Applied division of labor to its logical extreme

## Impact

- Made cars affordable: $850 (1908) → $260 (1925)
- 15 million Model T's produced by 1927
- Established mass production as dominant paradigm
- Influenced manufacturing far beyond automotive

## The Tradeoff

Efficiency and scale over flexibility and customization. Ford's famous quote: "Any customer can have a car painted any color that he wants so long as it is black."

## Historical Context

Taiichi Ohno studied Ford's methods before creating TPS. TPS kept the efficiency, added flexibility and quality. The Rouge Plant was the closest pre-computing analog to a dark factory: raw ore in, finished cars out.
