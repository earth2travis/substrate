# Jean Baudrillard: Sociologist of the Simulacrum

## Summary

Jean Baudrillard (1929–2007) was a French sociologist and philosopher whose analysis of media, consumer culture, and technological communication diagnosed the shift from representation to simulation. His most influential work, *Simulacra and Simulation* (1981), traces four stages of the image from reflection of reality to pure simulacrum with no original. The concept of hyperreality describes a surplus of reality generated by models. For AI systems, Baudrillard's framework is prophetic: LLMs generate speech without referent, producing hyperreal meaning that is more fluent than human output. DSJJJJ's Desideran AI can be read as a response to total simulation, building mischievous instability back in as the remainder that escapes the code.
