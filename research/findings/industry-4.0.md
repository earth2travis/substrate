# Industry 4.0

## Summary

Industry 4.0 is the fourth industrial revolution: smart manufacturing using cyber-physical systems, IoT, cloud computing, and AI to create interconnected, data-driven production environments. The progression of industrial revolutions runs from Industry 1.0 (mechanization via steam power) through Industry 2.0 (mass production and scientific management) and Industry 3.0 (automation via PLCs and CNC) to Industry 4.0 (cyber-physical integration and AI). Industry 4.0 enables mass customization at unprecedented scale: smart factories can produce personalized products on the same line as standardized ones, switching configurations automatically based on digital specs. This represents the convergence of lean principles with digital infrastructure.

## Key Claims

- Industry 4.0 combines cyber-physical systems, IoT, cloud/edge computing, AI, digital twins, and additive manufacturing
- It enables mass customization by automatically reconfiguring production lines based on digital specifications
- Digital twins allow simulation and optimization before physical production
- The dark factory (lights-out manufacturing) is the logical endpoint of Industry 4.0 automation
