# Nagarjuna's Mulamadhyamakakarika: Emptiness as Dependent Origination

## Summary

Nagarjuna's *Mulamadhyamakakarika* (c. 2nd century CE) is the foundational text of Madhyamaka Buddhism. Its central claim is that all phenomena are empty (*sunya*) of inherent existence (*svabhava*), and that this emptiness is identical with dependent origination (*pratityasamutpada*). The Catuskoti (tetralemma) negates all four positions on any proposition about inherent existence: neither A, nor not-A, nor both, nor neither. The two truths doctrine distinguishes conventional truth (functional, transactional) from ultimate truth (emptiness), insisting they are neither identical nor different. Emptiness itself is empty: to reify it is to fall into the same error. The soteriological purpose is liberation from clinging by seeing that nothing possesses the fixed essence grasping requires.
