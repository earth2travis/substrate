# Sources Bibliography: UX Research Scenarios

## Nielsen Norman Group (NNG)

1. **"Turn User Goals into Task Scenarios for Usability Testing"** — Hoa Loranger (2014). Foundational article establishing three core principles: realistic, actionable, non-leading. Includes before/after examples.

2. **"Write Better Qualitative Usability Tasks: Top 10 Mistakes to Avoid"** — Kathryn Whitenton (2017). Most comprehensive catalog of scenario anti-patterns. Each mistake includes example with correction.

3. **"From Research Goals to Usability-Testing Scenarios: A 7-Step Method"** — Page Laubheimer (2020). Complete upstream process from stakeholder concerns through problem statements to finished scenarios. Only source systematically connecting business goals to scenario writing.

4. **"Authentic Behavior in User Testing"** — Jakob Nielsen (2015). Explains WHY scenarios work psychologically (suspension of disbelief, engagement). Techniques for rescuing sessions when scenarios fail.

5. **"Usability Testing 101"** — Kate Moran (2019). Contextualizes scenarios within broader methodology. Defines relationship between facilitator, tasks, and participants.

6. **"Avoid Leading Questions to Get Better Insights from Participants"** — Kathryn Whitenton (2020). Leading questions and leading scenarios share the same root problem: injecting researcher bias. Framework for identifying priming effects.

## Books (Practitioner Canon)

7. **"About Face: The Essentials of Interaction Design"** — Cooper, Reimann, Cronin, Noessel (2014). Definitive source on goal-directed design scenarios. Introduces taxonomy of context scenarios, key path scenarios, validation scenarios.

8. **"Designing for the Digital Age"** — Kim Goodwin (2009). Most thorough practitioner treatment of scenarios. Adds environmental, emotional, and social context layers.

9. **"Making Use: Scenario-Based Design of Human-Computer Interactions"** — John M. Carroll (2000). Academic foundation. Originated the methodology in the 1990s. Theoretical framework others built upon.

10. **"Scenario-Based Design: Envisioning Work and Technology in System Development"** — John M. Carroll (1995). Collected foundational papers establishing scenarios as legitimate design methodology.

11. **"Rocket Surgery Made Easy"** — Steve Krug (2010). Most accessible practical guide. Emphasis on keeping scenarios simple and "tell me back" pilot testing.

12. **"Don't Make Me Think, Revisited"** — Steve Krug (2014). Practical guidance on writing test tasks.

13. **"The User Experience Team of One"** — Leah Buley (2013). Lean approaches for solo practitioners. Proto-persona to scenario workflow.

14. **"Mental Models"** — Indi Young (2008). Rigorous method for understanding how users think about a problem space, directly informing scenario writing.

15. **"Practical Empathy"** — Indi Young (2015). Techniques for understanding user motivations, essential input for scenarios reflecting real behavior.

## Academic

16. **"Five Reasons for Scenario-Based Design"** — John M. Carroll (1999). Concise articulation of why scenarios work: concrete, flexible, grounded, encourage stakeholder participation.

17. **"Usability Problem Description and the Evaluator Effect in Usability Testing"** — Molich, Ede, Halkjaer, Jensen (2004). Demonstrates how different evaluators using same scenarios find different problems. Implications for scenario specificity.

## Online Practitioner Resources

18. **Jeff Sauro / MeasuringU** — Quantitative approach bridging qualitative scenario writing with measurable outcomes (task success rates, time on task, error rates).

19. **Jared Spool / UIE** — Practical scenario guidance, particularly around testing information architecture. "Activity-focused design" aligns with scenario-based approaches.

20. **IDEO / d.school Design Thinking Resources** — Scenario-based methods in prototype and test phases. Emphasis on empathy-driven design informing scenario grounding in user research.
