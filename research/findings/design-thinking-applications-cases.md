# Design Thinking: Applications and Case Evidence

## Key Points

**The evidence pyramid inverts the publicity pyramid.** The most-cited cases (Bank of America's Keep the Change, GE's Adventure Series MRI, Airbnb's 2009 photography experiment) have the weakest independent verification. Keep the Change's 2.5M-customers-in-a-year figure is self-reported by IDEO and BofA; GE's pediatric sedation reductions circulate as GE/hospital retellings without a canonical peer-reviewed controlled study; Airbnb's photo trip is documented as an event, but its "design thinking" framing is retrospective founder narrative. The mechanism in each case is real and plausible; the success metrics trace to parties with the strongest incentive to tell a success story.

**Both flagship measurement instruments are correlational, one is additionally compromised.** McKinsey's Design Index (October 2018, verified): 300 companies, five years, top-quartile scorers "outperformed industry-benchmark growth by as much as two to one" (32pp revenue, 56pp TRS); McKinsey itself says "strong correlation," not causation. The DMI Design Value Index (2013: 228%; 2014: 219%; 2015: 211% over the S&P) is a backtested, hand-selected, 16-company theoretical portfolio, Microsoft-funded, Apple-weighted (138% ex-Apple in 2014), sold by a sponsor that sells design-management services. The honest summary: design-mature firms outperformed, not that design thinking causes outperformance.

**Government and healthcare cases are adoption evidence, not outcome evidence.** Denmark's MindLab (founded 2002, cross-ministerial, verified; wound down 2018-2019) diffused method worldwide but Bason's account is in-house. UK GDS (2011), USDS and 18F (2014), VA VEO (2015), Singapore and Helsinki labs prove design can be institutionalized at scale: playbooks published, staff trained, labs opened. Nearly all performance numbers are agency self-reports. Healthcare shows the same pattern: Mayo CFI, Kaiser Garfield Center, IDEO.org (founded 2011, verified) are institutional proofs, not controlled evaluations.

**Enterprise programs prove diffusion, not causation.** IBM (Loop, Hills, Sponsor Users; Forrester TEI claiming 301% ROI is IBM-commissioned), SAP (Fiori 2013, Plattner sponsorship), and Intuit D4D (Innovation Catalysts, Scott Cook) all show verifiable training scale. Outcome metrics are self-reported or commissioned. The training scale itself is the data point: the method spread.

**The Stanford Extreme Affordability cohort is the strongest existence proof.** Embrace infant warmer (2007 student project, nonprofit 2008) and d.light (100M+ people reached, partially corroborated by industry data) show a design course can produce durable ventures. The unit of evidence is ventures founded and units distributed, not health or income outcomes against controls.
