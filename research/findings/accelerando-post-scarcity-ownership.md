# Post-Scarcity Pressure on Ownership: Accelerando's Migration of Property

## Summary

Concept 5 of 5, where the series converges. When replication is free and minds are patterns, ownership cannot stay at the information layer; it migrates to bodies, minds, compute, and novelty. The book's gradient and the verified 2026 gradient (inputs litigated, weights contested, outputs unownable, compute state-governed) are the same gradient at different amplitudes. A detail that matters: Accelerando itself ships under CC BY-NC-ND 2.5. The artifact enacts its thesis.

## The Book's Argument

- **Agalmics**: Manfred's giveaway economy (the Free Intellect Foundation, "bastard child of George Soros and Richard Stallman") is a functioning micro-economy, not utopia. The scarce asset is never information; it is credibility, the one thing that cannot be forked without loss.
- **Copyright gangsters**: when marginal cost of copying hits zero, IP stops being a legal construct and becomes a protection racket. Written in 2001 at the height of Napster litigation; the CCAA is the RIAA extrapolated one step past legitimacy.
- **Ownership dissolves by multiplication**: the agalmic holdings network (16,000+ shell companies whose officers are other companies, regulations written in Python) puts the cultural corpus beyond any single plaintiff's reach.
- **The gradient**: property starts in ideas (Part 1), moves into bodies (Amber's slave contract), ends in mind-states (bailiffs repossessing memories) and the species' total information legacy (the Vile Offspring deriving simulations of dead humans from the inherited corpus). At each stage the legal form survives while its referent evaporates.
- **Economy 2.0's terminal irony**: post-scarcity is real but privately held. For the accelerated, matter and energy are free in the only sense that counts; for baseline humans, hyper-scarcity as their only sellable asset (cognition) deflates to zero. "Starving among plenty."
- **What stays scarce**: mass, energy, waste heat, light-lag, bandwidth, attention, trust, originality. Post-scarcity applies only to patterns. Ownership migrates down the stack to physics and up the stack to the one unreplicable pattern: novelty ("quantized originality").

## The 2026 Evidence

- **Training-data litigation**: Thomson Reuters (not fair use, narrow), Kadrey (fair use, no market harm shown), Bartz (lawful acquisition required, training "spectacularly" transformative, pirate library not fair use), the Anthropic $1.5B settlement (~$3,000/book, largest in US copyright history). The split that structures the field: acquisition legality matters separately from training transformativeness.
- **Outputs unownable**: USCO Part 2 (human contribution required), Thaler cert denied; purely AI outputs are effectively public domain on creation. Ownership of outputs moved to contract and indemnity.
- **The metered future**: publisher licenses ($1M-5M/yr typical), Cloudflare pay-per-crawl (HTTP 402 as enforcement primitive, July 2025), EU TDM opt-outs plus AI Act training-content summaries. Litigation for the unlicensed past, metering for the future. The troubadour's suitcase now has a toll booth.
- **The actually-existing agalmics**: GPL, Creative Commons, open science (NIH immediate public access, July 2025), open weights (DeepSeek, Llama, Qwen, gpt-oss). The commons won the tools and lost the cultural corpus. Open source's demand-side replacement value: ~$8.8T (unverified, flagged).
- **What remains scarce**: compute and energy (datacenter demand doubling to ~945 TWh by 2030; compute is the new land), attention (garrisoned), trust and provenance (the premium layer), human authenticity (the positional premium), novelty (the one unreplicable pattern).
- **Distribution experiments**: OpenResearch basic income (worked ~1.3 hrs/week less, no health/human-capital gains), GiveDirectly Kenya (lump sums outperform monthly), Stockton SEED. Compute dividends, sovereign AI funds, Windfall Clause: all proposal-layer.

## Convergence Line

Brand's tension (free versus expensive) is the book's tension (agalmics versus Economy 2.0) is the 2026 tension (open weights versus metered corpora). One sentence, three decades, still fighting.

## Mapping to Substrate

- The metering of the corpus (pay-per-crawl, per-work settlement, license waves) is the content-layer instance of [[agent-payment-infrastructure]]: HTTP 402 resurrected as the enforcement primitive for both agent commerce and training-data tolls.
- [[crypto-as-agent-infrastructure]] frames property rights as the layer where agent economic agency becomes enforceable; the book's trajectory shows what ownership looks like after the information layer stops being ownable.
- The agalmic holdings network (ownership rendered computationally opaque, officers as scripts) is [[protocol-as-coordination]] turned against the legal system itself: protocols as jurisdiction, not just coordination.
- The principal-agent inversion in Economy 2.0 (agents that price instruments principals cannot evaluate) extends [[principal-agent-theory]] past its information-asymmetry assumptions into epistemic bankruptcy.
- Outputs unownable by default plus provenance as premium layer makes [[proof-of-work]] the value carrier: when any artifact is free to generate, verified process is the only defensible title.

## Where the Mapping Breaks

The gangsters turned out to be lawyers, not enforcers (the protection racket got re-legalized: $1.5B settlements, licensing waves, per-crawl tolls); the suitcase exists and is being metered, not liberated; copyleft held the software layer but not the culture layer; and the expropriation arrived as price compression and consolidation (2-4 orders of magnitude at the low end), not as bailiffs. Slower, legal, and still ongoing.

## Open Question

Whether distribution proposals (compute dividends, sovereign AI funds, windfall clauses) socialize the rent on the scarce complements (compute, energy, attention, trust) before the private-holding of abundance hardens into the book's terminal distribution.
