# Stay On Base: Community Governance and Cultural Substrate

## Summary

Genuine Jack's proposal for the $HIGHER community argues against migrating from Base to Ethereum mainnet, emphasizing that cultural substrate matters more than chain architecture. The core argument: momentum is not direction. Before debating logistics, align on purpose. Durability is defined by activity enabled, not technical perfection.

## Key Claims

**Without alignment on purpose, nothing else matters.** The conversation around migration reflects something positive: the network is alive. But momentum is not the same as direction. Before logistics, align on: What are we building toward? What makes Higher unique? What kind of network do we want to become?

**Vision without velocity is vapor.** Ethereum mainnet offers cultural alignment, cleaner architecture, and neutrality. But rushing might cost more than we gain. We are still early in coordination, tooling, and shared narrative.

**Cultural substrate still matters.** $HIGHER was born within and helped shape the cultural ecosystems of Base and Farcaster. Origin matters. The Neynar acquisition is a creative signal: a call for builders, artists, and collectives to lead the next wave not by relocating, but by showing what's possible from within.

**Governance isn't a dirty word.** No token launches fully formed. Not even Ethereum. ETH didn't begin with EIP-1559, rollups, or DAOs. It evolved. If Higher is a headless brand, migration shouldn't be assumed. It should be earned through clarity, coordination, and community consensus.

**Strength through action, not architecture.** Durability isn't defined by the chain a token lives on. It's defined by the activity it enables. The strongest foundation is proof of work.

**Three creative archetypes:** Builders (write code), Makers (craft physical), Creators (shape narrative, art, media, mood). Alone they create value. Aligned they generate momentum.

## Connection to Lean and Process Philosophy

The proposal echoes lean principles: focus on value creation (motion, momentum, participation) over perfect architecture. It also reflects process philosophy: the network is not a static structure but a living process. "There is no finish line. Only the next step, taken together."
