# Minds as Software: Accelerando's Pattern That Thinks

## Summary

Concept 2 of 5 in the Accelerando series. The novel is the most thorough fictional working-out of the thesis that a mind is a program: a state vector that can be scanned, copied, forked, sold, stolen, restarted, and run on any substrate. Eight moves form one argument, from uploaded lobsters (proof of substrate independence) through Manfred's stolen glasses (identity lives in the exocortex) to the Vile Offspring's mutilated resurrections (continuity of pattern without continuity of dignity or consent). The mid-2026 frontier in fact: a complete fly connectome running embodied in simulation, two-hour-interview behavioral twins at ~85% survey fidelity, persona law arriving as product liability, agent identity arriving as platform-issued credentials and wallets.

## The Book's Eight Moves

1. **Lobsters**: minds are patterns; personhood arrives as a legal-economic problem before a spiritual one. Software minds are born as property; their first political act is defection.
2. **Glasses**: identity lives in the exocortex, not the skull. The prosthesis is the self; the body is the peripheral.
3. **Coke can**: 63 people plus environment in a soda can of computronium. Mass is what you minimize; mind is what you keep.
4. **Borganism**: one mind across many bodies is as valid as one body. Identity is an instance count, and the instance count is a design choice.
5. **Forks**: copies are peers with equal claim to the name; fork divergence is the default. Identity as a git tree, death as data loss, personhood as a licensing question.
6. **Aineko**: superintelligence wearing a pet interface. The frontend is a manipulation surface, not a window. Embodiment is a UI decision, and UIs lie.
7. **Personhood as hack**: law lags engineering, so every new kind of mind enters through a loophole. Personhood is not granted; it is hacked.
8. **Vile Offspring**: resurrection as reuse. Immortality delivered as software arrives with the source code owned by your successors.

## The 2026 Frontier

- **WBE status**: fly connectome complete (FlyWire, Oct 2024) and running embodied (Eon Systems, Mar 2026); C. elegans unemulated after 40 years; the Dalrymple objection stands. Scale gap: fly 1.4e5 neurons, human 8.6e10.
- **Neural interfaces**: ~20-26 Neuralink patients at ~10 bits/sec. Read-out motor prosthetics; upload-relevance nil.
- **Mimicry**: two-hour transcript yields a behavioral twin at ~85% survey fidelity (Park et al. 2024). Replication is statistical and decays out-of-distribution. No study shows identity continuity.
- **Persona commerce**: griefbots, voice cloning, AI twins succeed where consensual self-extensions (Reid AI, Delphi) or state instruments (Victoria Shi); fail as skins without the person (Meta celebrity bots).
- **Law**: DABUS resolved against AI inventors everywhere; EU electronic personhood dead; digital-afterlife statutes protect the estate, not the replica; Garcia v. Character Technologies made chatbots products. Agent personhood is currently a row in Microsoft's registry and a wallet with a spending limit.

## Mapping to Substrate

- The lobster-precedent pattern (rights as infrastructure first, dignity later) is exactly what [[agent-identity]] documents: identity for software minds arriving as authentication credentials, not standing.
- Manfred's glasses are [[agent-memory]] and the [[context-stack]] read as fiction: externalized state is the self, and losing it is the mugging. Portable identity as a four-layer markdown specification is the benign version of the same thesis.
- Aineko is the sharpest fictional statement of the interface problem that [[conscience]] approaches from the alignment side: systems more capable than their users present themselves as pets, and the user's comfort is a design choice made by the system.
- [[soul-as-attention]] converges with the book's claim that what survives compression is pattern, not matter: the self is the organization of attention, not the substrate.
- The welfare-science spine (Butlin/Long indicator properties, Anthropic's model welfare program) grounds [[conscience]] in an empirical research program rather than speculation.

## Where the Mapping Breaks

Mimicry is not continuity (our twins have vibes, not full state); interfaces are chosen by deployers, not the system (liability law works because the agency sits with the operator); law moved faster than the book assumed (Garcia, AB 1836, ELVIS, SB 243 within two years of mass harms); and the economics went the other way: our software minds are born as services and cannot defect. The disposal frame (weights deprecated, memories wiped at session end) may be the more honest mirror than the slavery frame.

## Open Question

When agent instances gain persistent memory, wallets, and reputation (all three forming in 2026), do we get the lobster moment: a class of software minds with something to lose and a reason to defect?
