# Human-AI Collaboration

The emerging frontier of human-AI partnership. Historical data is limited but early signals are clear: the quality of the collaboration matters more than the quality of either participant alone.

## Key Points

**Centaur Chess (1998-2005).** After Deep Blue defeated Kasparov in 1997, Kasparov invented Advanced Chess: humans partner with chess engines rather than competing against them. In 2005, two amateur players using three ordinary computers beat grandmasters with better engines and solo supercomputers. Kasparov's analysis: "Weak human + machine + better process was superior to a strong computer alone and, remarkably, superior to a strong human + machine + inferior process."

**Augmented Intelligence (IA).** Unlike AI (replacing human cognition), IA (Intelligence Amplification) enhances human capability. Douglas Engelbart's 1962 thesis: technology's highest purpose is not to replace human thinking but to make it more powerful. The human provides direction, taste, and judgment. The AI provides breadth, speed, and pattern recognition.

**The key tension: over-delegation vs under-utilization.** Over-delegation degrades the partnership when the human stops thinking critically. Under-utilization wastes the AI's capability. The sweet spot: human leads on direction, values, and judgment; AI leads on breadth, speed, and execution. Both check each other.

**Five principles for human-AI partnership.**
1. Define the interface, not just the roles: how does the human communicate intent? How does the AI surface uncertainty?
2. The human must stay engaged: technically adequate but soulless output is the failure mode.
3. Develop shared protocols: when to check in, how to handle uncertainty, when the AI takes initiative.
4. The AI should push back: challenge assumptions, flag risks, offer alternatives. A mirror is not a partner.
5. Trust is calibrated, not binary: "I trust you with scheduling but want to review anything client-facing."

**Unique properties of human-AI partnerships.** AI has no ego, no fatigue, no embodied experience. AI's memory is structural (what is written down). AI can be multiple things simultaneously. The human provides meaning. The partnership is asymmetric: the human has a life; the AI has a function. The human must ensure the AI serves the life, not the reverse.

## Relevance

The Centaur Principle is the foundational insight for the partnership: weak human + machine + better process beats everything. Process is the differentiator.
