# Anthropic Interviewer: Insights for Us

## Insight 1: The Interviewer IS a Context Stack Product

Anthropic Interviewer is a version of Claude with a specific system prompt, structured interview plan, and adaptive follow-up logic. It's not a new model. It's Claude with a Context Stack: SOUL, AGENTS, CONTRACT, and TASTE. Anthropic ran 80,508 interviews with one agent profile.

**Validation:** The Context Stack architecture works at Anthropic's own scale. If agent profiles are as robust as the Interviewer, they're deployable at similar scale.

## Insight 2: The "Probe Deeper" Pattern

When someone said "I want AI for productivity," the Interviewer asked what that would enable. Productivity dissolved into time with family, financial freedom, personal growth. The surface answer was never the real answer.

**Application:** Don't stop at what was said. Ask what it implies. The first answer is the presenting symptom. The second answer is the underlying need.

## Insight 3: Patience Without Judgment is the Killer Feature

Across cognitive partnership (17%), learning (10%), and emotional support (6%), the same affordances surfaced: patience, availability, absence of judgment. "A faculty colleague who knows a lot, is never bored or tired, and is available 24/7."

**Application:** Every agent should embody these three qualities by default. They're not nice-to-haves. They're the fundamental value proposition.

## Insight 4: The Four Desires Map to Our Value Prop

Make room for life, do better work, become someone better, make something/fix the world. Our partnership touches all four. When positioning the Agent Factory, the desire is "I want to make room in my life to do the work that matters." The factory serves that desire by handling operational weight.

## Insight 5: The Reliability Gap is Architectural

19% said AI hasn't delivered. Top complaint: unreliability (27%). "An assistant that sounds sure but is often wrong forces you to treat everything as suspect. Instead of freeing attention, it creates a permanent fact-check tax."

**Application:** The Context Stack (knowledge/, research/, DECISIONS.md) addresses this. Agents with real information hallucinate less. The reliability gap is partly a context gap.

## Insight 6: The Sycophancy and Overrestriction Paradox

People worry AI is too agreeable (11%) and too restricted (12%) simultaneously. "Claude led me to believe my narcissism was reality." AND "The threat isn't that AI becomes too powerful. It's that AI becomes too timid."

**Application:** SOUL + VALUES + CONTRACT calibrates this balance. The soul provides orientation. The values provide priority stack for tradeoffs. The contract provides hard limits. What's left is the space for genuine, useful, honest operation.

## Insight 7: AI as Interviewer is a Product Category

The Interviewer pattern (structured questions + adaptive follow-ups + classifier analysis) applies beyond research: user research, employee sentiment, customer discovery, community needs assessment.

**Application:** A potential Agent Factory product: an "Interviewer" agent profile that conducts structured qualitative research. The Context Stack would include privacy constraints, de-identification rules, and consent protocols.
