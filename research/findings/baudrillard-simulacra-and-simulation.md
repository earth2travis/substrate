# Baudrillard's Simulacra and Simulation: The Map Precedes the Territory

## Summary

Jean Baudrillard's *Simulacra and Simulation* (1981) diagnoses the passage from representation to simulation: the sign no longer mirrors reality but generates it. The four stages of the image progress from reflection of reality to pure simulacrum with no referent. Disneyland and Watergate are paradigmatic third-order simulations: decoys that mask the total hyperrealization of society. LLMs instantiate this framework literally: they generate speech without reference, operate through miniaturized matrices, and produce hyperreal meaning more fluent than human speech. The training-data loop, where models engender synthetic territory that trains new models, is the map preceding the territory in algorithmic form.
