**Karpathy: From Vibe Coding to Agentic Engineering** (Sequoia Capital). Karpathy discussing the transition from casual exploratory coding with AI to structured, goal-directed agent work. His framing of this transition likely establishes vocabulary and best practices. Karpathy is the most credible voice in applied AI engineering. **AI State of the Union: Dark Factories Are Coming** (Lenny's Podcast). The claim that we have passed the inflection point and dark factories are coming. Dark factories are fully automated, AI-run manufacturing and operations. The inflection point claim suggests a phase change in AI adoption. The [[dark-factory]] concept in the Substrate covers the lights-out manufacturing pattern.

**The Layer Almost Everyone's Missing** (The AI Automators). Discussion of what comes after the harness, the layer above harnesses. This could be directly relevant to Mission Contracts as the orchestration layer.
