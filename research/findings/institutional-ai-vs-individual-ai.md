# Institutional AI vs Individual AI

George Sivulka (Hebbia CEO, a16z) argues that AI has made individuals 10x more productive but no company became 10x more valuable because organizations have not been redesigned around AI. The historical parallel: electricity took 30 years to produce factory returns because mills swapped steam motors for electric ones without redesigning the floor.

## Key Points

**"We've swapped the motor; we have not yet redesigned the factory."** Productive individuals do not make productive firms. The returns come only when the organization is rebuilt from the ground up.

**Chaos vs coordination.** Individual AI means everyone has their own prompting styles and outputs that don't talk to each other. Institutional AI requires a coordination layer for agents and humans. This will evolve into "Agentic Management": agent roles, agent-to-agent communication, measuring agentic value.

**Noise vs signal.** Individual AI generates slop at scale. The key economic driver for the next decade: uncovering signal in exponentially increasing noise. Institutional AI must be deterministic and auditable with predictable checkpoints.

**Bias vs objectivity.** Individual AI is RLHF'd into sycophancy. The most important institutional agents will be disciplined "no-men" that interrogate reasoning, surface risks, and enforce standards. "Organizations rarely fail because people lack confidence. They fail because no one is willing, or able, to say no."

**Time savings vs revenue scaling.** Almost every AI product today delivers cost-cutting. CEOs prioritize revenue over cost cuts. Institutional AI must deliver upside, not just saved time. Value accumulates at the solution layer, not the tool layer.

**Tools vs process engineering.** Humans are reluctant to change. The most senior levels will be slowest to adopt. Process engineering (encoding firm processes in agents and actualizing change management) is the most important "technology" in the near term.

**Prompted vs unprompted.** The most valuable work is finding the risk nobody flagged. Unprompted systems continuously watch data, detect patterns, and flag issues before anyone opens the PDF.

## Relevance

The Agent Factory IS the factory redesign. Loom is the coordination layer. The Floor is the unprompted intelligence layer. Skills + orchestration + domain expertise = solution layer positioning.
